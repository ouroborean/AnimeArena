# Patch 2026-08-02

**44 characters · 77 changes.**

> [!warning] Status: drafted, not live
> These notes were written from the owner's change list and the code recon behind
> [[Patch 2026-08-02 - Implementation Roadmap]]. Nothing here has been implemented yet, and a handful
> of lines are still waiting on an owner decision (they are marked **pending** and listed in the
> roadmap's open questions). Do not publish this page to players until the roadmap's Phase 9 is green.

## What this patch is about

Two threads run through it.

**Absolutes get trimmed.** Five characters lose a blanket immunity or an absolute they have been
carrying: Nimaiya's Invulnerability bypass, Midoriya's Stun immunity, Meliodas's blanket ignore on
negative non-damage effects, Mavis's Invulnerability while sealed in Fairy Heart, and Yubel's
permanent Isolate. None of them is replaced with a weaker version of the same thing — the clause is simply gone.

**Two Stuns become something Stun immunity does not answer.** Yugi's *Swords of Revealing Light* and Esdeath's *Mahapadma* stop being Stun effects. They now **prevent skills from being used** and
**cannot be ignored** — no Stun immunity, no "unstunnable" skill, no shrug-off clears them. Yugi's
version only prevents **Harmful** skills, and his own Dark Magician and Dark Magician Girl are exempt.

Alongside those, a broad energy and cooldown pass touches 19 skills across 15 characters, and Soul Evans, Tsubaki, Jaden and the Thompson Sisters get reworks rather than tuning.
