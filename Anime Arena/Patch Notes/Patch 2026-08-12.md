# Patch 2026-08-12

**6 characters · 9 skill changes**, plus a new Nexus concept, a UI addition, and two color-filter fixes.

> [!warning] Status: drafted, not live
> These changes are in the working tree. Server-side ability logic requires a server restart to take
> effect; the client bundle (`webclient/app` → `deploy/`) is synced.

## What this patch is about

A pass on a few over/under-tuned kits. **Escanor**'s Cruel Sun stops handing out free Sunshine and
retunes toward rewarding aggression; his Sunshine is no longer immune to cleansing. **Tsubasa**'s
Burning Wrath Whirl is rebuilt from a fiddly three-use combo into a clean Channeled AoE damage-over-time
that transforms into Burning Wrath Blade. **Byakuya**'s Senka becomes an immediate, visible tempo hit,
and his Bankai now swaps it out for White Imperial Sword. Plus small dials on Android 17, Stark, and
Machinedramon — and Kyoko Sakura joins the Nexus.

---

## Android 17

- **Buff** *Android Assault* — damage **10 → 15**. Its damage now lasts the same cost-scaled window as
  the cost increase, so when the skill's cost is raised the **whole** effect becomes multi-turn (the
  damage ticks each turn), not just the cost tax.
- **Buff** *Barrier* — Shield **20 → 25** (the +10-per-Random-energy scaling is unchanged).

## Byakuya

- **Rework** *Senka* — now **Instant** and no longer invisible. It immediately deals **15 Piercing**
  damage and **permanently** lowers the target's damage by **5** (was: a delayed, invisible effect that
  only resolved if the target didn't act).
- **Change** *Bankai: Senbonzakura Kageyoshi* — triggering Bankai now replaces **Senka** with White
  Imperial Sword (was: Bakudo #61: Six-Rod Light Restraint). Bakudo #61 stays usable during Bankai.

## Escanor

- **Rework** *Cruel Sun* — no longer grants a free stack of Sunshine each turn, and no longer reduces
  affected enemies' non-Affliction damage. Instead, **while Cruel Sun is active, Escanor gains 1 Sunshine
  whenever he uses a Harmful skill**. It still deals **5 Affliction** to all enemies each turn for **4
  turns**.
- **Change** *Sunshine* — Sunshine stacks can now be **cleansed** (e.g. by Inuyasha's Iron Reaver Soul
  Stealer). (was: could not be cleansed)

## Machinedramon

- **Buff** *EMP Wave* — now also grants Machinedramon **1 Blue energy**.

## Stark

- **Nerf** *To The Rescue* — cooldown **1 → 2**.

## Tsubasa

- **Rework** *Burning Wrath Whirl* — no longer a three-use staged combo. It is now a **Channeled** AoE
  damage-over-time dealing **10 Affliction to all enemies each turn for 3 turns**; on the **first turn**
  it also **stuns all enemies' non-Strategic skills for 1 turn**, and on its **final tick** it replaces
  itself with **Burning Wrath Blade** for 1 turn. Channeling ends early — with **no** Blade — if Tsubasa
  is stunned or uses another skill.

---

## Nexus

- **New** *Kyoko Sakura* (Madoka Magica) added as a Nexus concept.

## Quality of life

- The in-battle character-info panel (shown when you click a character) now displays that character's
  **universe**.

## Fixes

- **Yumiya Rakko** and **Hibiki Tachibana** now appear under the correct **color filter** — both use
  **White** energy (Rakko was under no color; Hibiki was under Green/Red). *(Escanor was also corrected
  from Blue/Red to Green/Red.)*
