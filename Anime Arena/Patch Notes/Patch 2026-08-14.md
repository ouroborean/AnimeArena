# Patch 2026-08-14
**2 new characters and a Ladder overhaul**, plus bounty, bypass, and UI work. The
headliners are **Piccolo** (Dragon Ball) and **Mash Burnedead** (Mashle), alongside a reworked Ladder
that stops separating a small player base by rank.

## What this patch is about
The player base isn't large enough to gate the Ladder by rank, so ranked matchmaking now pairs anyone
with anyone and simplifies how rating moves. **Piccolo** joins as a Channeling specialist and **Mash
Burnedead** as an enhance-focused striker. Clans gain an **Officer** role between leader and member.
And a batch of fixes lands — most importantly, Bypassing skills now actually apply their effects to the
invulnerable enemies they can already hit.

## New Characters
### Piccolo — Dragon Ball
A **Channeling** specialist. Three of his skills begin a channel that builds a stack every turn; he
**re-uses** the skill to release the payoff, and the longer he channels the more he's rewarded.

- **New** *Special Beam Cannon* — begins Channeling (+1 stack/turn). Re-use: **25 Piercing** to one
  enemy, +20 per stack.
- **New** *Hellzone Grenade* — begins Channeling, placing a stack on every targetable enemy each turn.
  Re-use for 1 Random: **10 damage per stack** to each enemy.
- **New** *Regeneration* — begins Channeling (+1 stack/turn). Re-use for free: heal **half his missing
  HP + 10 per stack**.
- **New** *Energy Deflection* — Invulnerable for 1 turn; usable mid-channel without ending it.
- **New** *Namekian Power* (passive) — whenever a channel ends he gains **5 permanent Shield for each
  turn he spent channeling** and that skill goes on cooldown 1. If an **enemy interrupts** the channel
  (Stun/Seal) he instead gains **1 Green energy**; using a different skill ends it with no payoff.

### Mash Burnedead — Mashle
An **enhance** melee striker who buffs his own strikes and shrugs off energy tricks. He sets up with Big
Bang Dash, then follows with an empowered Knuckle that tears through the enemy's defenses.

- **New** *Big Bang Dash* — free. Through his next turn it **enhances Ballista Knuckle and Hell Fall
  Knuckle** and makes Mash **ignore negative non-damage effects** for the turn.
- **New** *Ballista Knuckle* — **20 damage**. While enhanced, it first **shatters the target's Shield**,
  dealing **+10** if the target had one.
- **New** *Hell Fall Knuckle* — **25 damage**. While enhanced, it **strips the target's Shield and
  Damage Reduction**, dealing **+10 for each** it removes (up to +20).
- **New** *Playing With Magic* — Invulnerable for 1 turn.
- **New** *Muscle Magic* (passive) — Mash's energy **can't be drained or stolen** by enemies, and he
  **can't have Nullify applied** to him.

## Ladder
The big one — matchmaking and rating are reworked for a small, close-knit ladder.

- **Change** *Rank matching lifted* — players of **any rank now match with any other player**. The old
  system made far-apart players wait out the bot timer and meet a bot instead; with a small player
  base that just kept people from playing each other.
- **Change** *Rating no longer scales by opponent rank* — you gain and lose rating based on **your own
  rank**, period. No more bonus for beating someone higher or extra loss for losing to someone lower.
- **Buff** *Minimum ±10 rating per match* — high-rated players no longer crawl up 2–3 points at a time;
  every Ladder result now moves your rating by **at least 10**.
- **Buff** *Rating gains rescaled to the full ladder* — with ranks spread 1000 rating apart (Iron →
  Challenger), the old gain curve collapsed to the ±10 floor by **Silver**, so most of the climb paid
  ~10–16 a win no matter who you beat. The curve now falls gently through the low and mid ladder and only
  drops off near the top: a win is worth roughly **100 in Iron, ~95 through Silver, ~85 in Gold, ~70 in
  Platinum**, tapering through Diamond/Master to **~10 at Grandmaster** (and the ±10 floor beyond).
  Ranking up no longer halves your gains — you keep 60–70+ per win until around Platinum. Losses scale
  the same way, so a 50% record still holds steady.
- **Change** *Ranked bot timer 20s → 30s* — the wait before the Ladder seats a bot for you is longer,
  giving real opponents more time to appear.
- **Buff** *Ladder-bot losses capped* — a loss to a fallback bot can no longer cost more than a bot win
  could gain (both are capped at 25), so bot games can't quietly bleed your rating at low ratings.
- **Fix** *Win-rate rating bonus now works* — the performance bonus for high-win-rate players (up to
  +15%) had been silently inactive due to a math bug; it now applies as intended.

## Quality of life
- **Change** *Mobile character info panel* — tapping a character now **pops the skills panel up pinned
  to the top of the screen** and adds a **close button**, so you no longer have to scroll all the way
  up to read a character's skills and back down to keep browsing.
- **Clearer bounty instructions** — the bounty panel now spells out that you only
  need to complete a **single row, column, or diagonal** to finish a Bounty, and that you can spend AP
  to finish individual missions (scaled by remaining wins).

## Fixes
- **Fix** *Bypassing skills now affect invulnerable targets* — several skills that can be **used** on an
  invulnerable enemy were silently failing to apply their **effect** to that enemy. Fixed for **Mine's
  Genius Sniper**, **Cell's Inherited Death Beam**, **Yubel's Malicious Intent**, and **Yuno's Spirit of
  Zephyr**.
- **Fix** *Color filter* — corrected the energy colors for **12 characters** so they appear under the
  right filters: Astolfo, BlackWarGreymon, Cell, Chrome Dokuro, Cooler, Crona, Satoru Gojo, Hisoka,
  Mayuri Kurotsuchi, Sasuke, Sailor Uranus, and Yubel. (New characters now derive their filter colors
  from their actual ability costs automatically.)
- **Fix** *Mine — High Output Blast Blade* — the ability's animation now plays with the corrected audio.
- **Fix** *Gatomon — Gatomon Digivolve* — the skill has always been usable while stunned, but its card
  never said so; it's now correctly tagged **Unstunnable**.
- **Fix** *Midoriya — Faux 100%* — the effect already made his skills **bypass Invulnerability**, but its
  tooltip only advertised the invuln-on-skill-use half; the tooltip now states the bypass as well.
