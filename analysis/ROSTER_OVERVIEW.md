## Energy-color distribution

Game uses 4 real colors (White/Blue/Red/Green) + Random/Colorless; no fifth color exists. Color presence across energy_identity (undercounts since profiles list representative colors): Blue 78 (45%), Red 75 (43%), Green 66 (38%), White 64 (37%) - fairly even, Blue slightly favored as the most common single identity. Random/Colorless surface kits are rare (~5, e.g. Ash 4xRandom, Shokuhou). Color breadth: ~85 mono-color, ~63 dual, ~21 three-or-four-color rainbow. Most kits commit 1-2 specific-color pips and pad with Random, so true mono-color lock (e.g. Horohoro 6/8 Blue) is the exception that drives color-screw risk.

## Design-space gaps (Phase-3 seeds)

- Dedicated tank/peel archetype is thin (~18 tagged tank, often secondary) - few characters whose primary job is soaking and protecting allies.
- Cleanse is scarce (22/172) and anti-cleanse counterplay rarer still, yet many top kits rely on permanent/uncleansable effects - the counter axis is underbuilt.
- Hard energy-denial is uncommon (30/172) and mostly soft; with low cooldowns dominating, resource-attrition as a win condition is underexplored (Uraraka tries but is bugged/weak).
- Stealth/information-warfare is a tiny niche (12 stealth, 7 invisible-tech); reveal/anti-stealth tools are nearly absent.
- AoE is bimodal: a handful at 75-125 and a large 0-AoE block, with little mid-range sustained AoE - few spread-chip midline kits.
- True colorless/Random-flex deployers are rare (~5); most kits are mono/dual color, leaving the splashable-flex lane open.
- Pure team-healer/support identity is underrepresented relative to self-sustain; team-wide sustained healing is rare versus self-heal/lifesteal.
- DoT is concentrated in a few specialists (Lucy 65, Maka 50) with most at 0; a mid-tier attrition/DoT band is sparse.
- Slow/high-cooldown big-payoff design barely exists (~15 gated kits) - the cooldown axis is an unused balance lever.
- Low-burst pure-controllers (Shokuhou, Yubel, Uraraka) are few and rate at extremes (great or unplayable) - the controllable midground is thin.

## Cross-cutting observations

- Implementation debt is the biggest theme: ~22 characters are unimplemented, stubbed, or carry dead-code/bugged passives (Guts, Snow White, Ruler, La Pucelle, Urahara, Ash, Gaia, Pyrrha, Omni-Man source-missing, Uraraka, Yumiya Rakko, Touka, Zenitsu). Many low isolation ratings are depressed by bugs, not design - balance and engineering passes must be sequenced together.
- 171/172 carry at least one outlier_flag, so the data is best read as where-to-look, not what-is-broken; recurring flag types are instant-kill/execute (~40), uncapped/unbounded scaling (~73 mention growth ceilings), and JSON-vs-.gd metadata staleness (Naruto, Hashirama, Nonon, Meliodas, Misaka, Sheele, Asui).
- Instant-kill/execute is a systemic balance axis, not an edge case: ~40 characters reference an execute, several unconditional and defense-bypassing (Saitama, Ichibe, Akame, Kurapika, Hisoka, Yugi, Rob Lucci, Iron Maiden Jeanne). These define the format ceiling and counterplay needs more than raw damage does.
- Piercing/affliction/bypass damage (40+ piercing, 11 affliction) is widespread, so listed peak numbers undersell effective damage versus tanky comps because so much output ignores DR/shields/invuln - tank and shield archetypes are structurally pressured.
- Cooldowns are nearly free roster-wide (146 fast/spammable), so the real cost knobs are energy pips and color requirements; balance levers should target cost and effect magnitude rather than cooldown length.
- Self-protection is assumed baseline (91% have invuln/counter), inflating effective survivability across the board and making the rare no-defense kits (pure controllers, fragile pokers) disproportionately weak in isolation.
- Ratings are tightly center-massed (119/172 at 5.5-6.5), so the mid-roster is reasonably level; Phase-2 effort is best spent on the ~20 tails - over-tuned execute/uncapped-scaling kits at the top and broken/unimplemented kits at the bottom - rather than the bulk.
